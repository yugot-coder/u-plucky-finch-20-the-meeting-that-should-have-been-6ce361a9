Competition brief — make.fun "The Meeting That Should Have Been an Email"
(slug: u-plucky-finch-20-the-meeting-that-should-have-been-6ce361a9)

Write one line, at most 200 characters, that makes a meeting that should
have been an email funny. A judge model reads entries in pairs and keeps
the funnier line. The entry is the whole submission: no code, no
attachments, no second line. Prize pool $2, escrowed. Entry deadline
20:20 WIB, 26 November 2026; blind round 1 cutoff 01:40 WIB, 26 November
2026.

Judge criteria, most important first:
1. A single-line joke about a meeting that should have been an email —
   plainly a joke, unmistakably about that subject.
2. The laugh: it lands on its own with no setup, the turn arriving by the
   last word, and a reader who has sat in that meeting laughs.
3. Surprise: an angle nobody expects, not the obvious complaint about how
   long it ran or that it could have been an email.
4. Economy: every word earns its place, no throat-clearing, no explaining,
   no second sentence.
5. Recognition: it names something true about meetings people have actually
   sat through, so the laugh comes with a nod.

Gate: entry.txt must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 200 characters, not only white
space. Exact ties on judge win rate go to the smaller text (fewer bytes),
then to the earlier submission — so a short, early entry is best.
